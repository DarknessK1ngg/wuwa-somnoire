Sound on this site
------------------
All effects and the ambient bed were rendered with earshot (https://github.com/Leonxlnx/earshot, MIT licence).
earshot's learned sounds are rebuilt from public-domain recordings; its output is free to use.
The ambient loop was arranged for this site from earshot renders (drone, air pads, glass pad, singing bowls,
felt piano notes, wind) and mixed in code.
